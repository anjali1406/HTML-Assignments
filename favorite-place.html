Ooty - My Favorite Place



About the Place

Ooty is one of my favorite places in Tamil Nadu. It is also known as Udhagamandalam and is famous for its beautiful hills, cool weather, tea gardens and peaceful surroundings.

I would love to visit Ooty because it is a perfect place to relax, enjoy nature and spend quality time with family and friends.


Why I Like Ooty

I like Ooty because of its beautiful scenery, pleasant climate and peaceful atmosphere.
